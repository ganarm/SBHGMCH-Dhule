About Us

Training College of Nursing

Training College of Nursing, established in 1996, is a premier nursing institution affiliated with S.B.H.G.M.C.H. Dhule. We are committed to producing competent, compassionate and skilled nursing professionals who can meet the evolving healthcare needs of the nation.

Our college is recognized by the Indian Nursing Council (INC) and the Maharashtra Nursing Council (MNC). We offer diploma, undergraduate and postgraduate programmes in nursing.

Our Hospital

Clinical Training & Healthcare Exposure

Students gain hands-on experience at S.B.H.G.M.C.H. Dhule — our parent hospital.

S.B.H.G.M.C.H. Dhule (Shri Bhausaheb Hire Government Medical College & Hospital) is our parent hospital, providing students with extensive clinical exposure across all major departments.

  • Hospital Training — Rotations in Medicine, Surgery, Paediatrics, Obstetrics & Gynaecology
  • Clinical Practice — Supervised patient care under experienced consultants
  • Community Health Visits — Rural and urban outreach programmes
  • Simulation Laboratory — Advanced simulation for emergency and critical care
  • Emergency Care Training — Hands-on experience in emergency and trauma care
